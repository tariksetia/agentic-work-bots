# What is an AI teammate?

An AI teammate is AI software that takes responsibility for an outcome rather than answering a single prompt. You describe what finished looks like. The teammate plans the steps, gathers context from the tools your team already uses, does the work, pauses for approval when an action needs human authority, and reports back where the team already talks.

That is different from opening a chatbot, writing a careful prompt, copying the answer and finishing the job yourself. A chatbot gives you an answer. An AI teammate does the work.

People also search for the same idea as an AI employee or an AI coworker. The labels overlap. What matters is the behavior:

- It works toward an outcome, not a single reply.

- It works across tools, such as a CRM, calendar, documents, analytics and messages.

- It uses team context, such as how the company describes its product and who approves external messages.

- It knows when to ask: sending, paying, deleting and publishing wait for a person.

# AI teammate vs chatbot, copilot and automation

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Type** | **What it returns** | **Your role** |
| Chatbot | An answer or a draft | Direct every step and finish the task |
| Copilot | Suggestions while you work | Stay involved throughout |
| Automation | The same predefined action each time | Build and maintain the workflow |
| **AI teammate** | **A finished result or an approval request** | **Set direction and review important actions** |

A chatbot is often the fastest way to answer a one-off question. A fixed automation suits a stable process with predictable inputs. An AI teammate fits work with a repeatable goal that still needs judgment, context or movement across several tools, such as a weekly pipeline report, account research or meeting follow-ups.

# What should stay human?

An AI teammate earns the name by taking responsibility for useful work while leaving consequential decisions with the people accountable for them.

Keep a human approval step for:

- External communication

- Payments and billing changes

- Deletions and access changes

- Public publishing

- Contracts and policies

- Commitments to customers or partners

The teammate can collect evidence, prepare options and recommend a next step. The accountable person makes the decision. When something fails, the right behavior is to stop, explain the problem and ask for direction.

# FAQ: AI teammates

#### What is an AI teammate?

An AI teammate is AI software that takes a goal, works across your business tools, completes multi-step work and asks for approval before consequential actions, then reports back in your team chat.

#### Is an AI teammate the same as an AI employee or an AI coworker?

Mostly, yes. Vendors use all three labels for AI that does work rather than only answering questions. Compare behavior: where it works, what it can act on, and which actions wait for approval.

#### What is the best AI teammate for Slack?

Dash and Viktor both work in Slack and Microsoft Teams and act across business tools. Lindy has a Slack-native teammate for personal assistant work, and Slackbot adds AI inside Slack on Business+ plans.

#### What is the best AI teammate for Microsoft Teams?

Dash and Viktor work directly in Microsoft Teams. Microsoft 365 Copilot is the choice for AI inside Office apps and Teams meetings, and many teams run it alongside an AI teammate.

#### How is an AI teammate different from a chatbot or a copilot?

A chatbot returns an answer and a copilot makes suggestions while you work. An AI teammate returns a finished result or an approval request.

#### Is there an AI teammate without per-seat pricing?

Dash is priced per workspace with no per-seat fees, and Viktor and Tasklet use shared credit pools. Lindy, Slackbot, Microsoft 365 Copilot and ChatGPT Business are priced per user.
