### How does Ask Buzz find prospects?

AskBuzz is in Beta. It uses AI to search across 350M+ verified contacts from 100+ data providers. Simply describe your ideal customer in natural language, and our AI will find perfect matches with verified emails and direct dials.

### What channels does the AI employee support?

Ask Buzz orchestrates multichannel outreach including email, LinkedIn, call, and voicemail drops. The AI sequences touches across channels based on prospect engagement and response patterns.

### How is this different from other sales tools?

Unlike traditional tools, Ask Buzz is truly agentic. Instead of simply automating tasks, it actually thinks. The AI researches each prospect, crafts hyper-personalized messages, and adapts its approach based on real-time engagement signals.

### Do I need a credit card to start?

No! You can start with free leads included, no credit card required. Explore the platform, run your first campaigns, and upgrade when you're ready to scale.

### How does waterfall enrichment work?

Waterfall enrichment cascades your data through multiple providers to maximize coverage. If one source doesn't have an email, we try the next, then the next — achieving 3× better coverage than single-source tools.

### Can I integrate with my existing CRM?

Absolutely. Ask Buzz integrates with 50+ tools including Salesforce, HubSpot, Outreach, Salesloft, and more. Enriched data syncs automatically, and you can manage everything from Slack if you prefer.
