I take action in your most used apps, without you ever leaving the conversation. You ask right where you’re working, and the work gets done.

Any tool can show you a chart. Ask me for a report or dashboard and I’ll build one right into the conversation by pulling in your data. You can sort, filter, or pin it to a channel so your whole team works from the same live view.

### Think it, say it, knock it out

How is Slackbot different from other AI tools?

Slackbot is the only AI that already knows your org — including your channels, permissions, history, and connected systems — from day one, with no setup required. Tag it into a channel, and it doesn’t just answer there. Slackbot is already grounded across every public channel you have access to, so it builds on what your team has already solved without you asking twice. Skills turn individual wins into workflows that the whole org can run instantly, so your whole team gets better together.

What can Slackbot help me with?

Slackbot helps you navigate your workday, doing the work that once required a specialist — synthesizing research, turning your data into a live dashboard, deck, or report the whole team can act on together, running Skills your team built, taking action across your apps, or building together with Slackbot in the channel. All from one conversation, without leaving Slack.

How does Slackbot protect the security of our data?

Slackbot runs natively inside your existing security perimeter and inherits your organization’s permissions, governance, and access controls automatically. Your data is never used to train AI models. Every agentic action gets a full audit trail and human-in-the-loop approval, so nothing runs without your say. And it's all backed by FedRAMP, SOC 2, EKM, DLP, and HIPAA compliance.

Does Slackbot work globally?

Yes, Slackbot can be used anywhere Slack is available. It respects your Slack language settings and can generate responses in the language you use in Slack.

How does Slackbot help with customer relationship management?

Slackbot puts your CRM where work happens. Update records, prep for meetings, log calls, and generate follow-ups without leaving Slack. Turn your pipeline, forecast, or case backlog into a live dashboard your whole team can act on right in the channel, no analyst or BI team required. And because Slackbot lives in the channel, everyone’s working from the same view, not a private spreadsheet only one person can see. For small businesses, Slack CRM lets you manage customers and accounts directly in Slack.

Which plan do I need to use Slackbot?

Slackbot is available on Business+ and Enterprise+, with unlimited access on Enterprise+. Business+ users have ongoing access with a weekly message limit, while Free and Pro users can try Slackbot out with a limited sample.

What apps and agents can Slackbot work with?

Slackbot connects to the tools your team already uses, including Jira, Figma, Salesforce, Docusign, and many more via MCP. You can also build custom skills to automate recurring work and share them with your whole team — no developer required.
