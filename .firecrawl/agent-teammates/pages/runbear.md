### What is an AI teammate for Slack?

An AI teammate is an AI agent that works inside your Slack channels like a colleague. Teammates @mention it or message it directly, and you can also let it reply to keywords or to every new message in a channel. It answers from your docs and takes actions in your tools. A Runbear agent is shared by the whole team, so everyone works with the same instructions, knowledge, and history.

### How fast can a team set up an AI teammate in Slack?

About 10 minutes. Describe the agent in plain English, connect the tools it needs from 2,000+ integrations, and install Runbear from the Slack Marketplace. An ops manager or team lead can do it with no engineering team, no server to host, and no Slack app to build.

### What can a Runbear agent do in Slack?

It answers questions from Notion, Google Drive, Confluence, and SharePoint, creates and updates records in tools like Linear, Zendesk, HubSpot, and Salesforce, and routes requests to the right person. On the Claude Agent SDK runtime, the usual default, you choose each agent's model from Claude, GPT, and Gemini; other runtimes offer a narrower set.

### Does Runbear work in Microsoft Teams too?

Yes. The same agents can be deployed to Slack and Microsoft Teams, so teams on both chat tools share one set of instructions, knowledge, and integrations.

### Is Runbear priced per seat?

No. Runbear charges by usage, not by seat. One subscription covers every teammate who uses your agents in Slack or Microsoft Teams. Plans include a set number of builders, the members of your Runbear organization who create and manage agents in the dashboard: 5 on Team and 10 on Business. Usage is metered in credits, based on the tokens each model call uses and the model you choose for each agent. Team is $99 per month with 20,000 credits, and Business is $399 per month with 100,000 credits.

### Is Runbear secure enough for company data?

SOC 2 Type II in production. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit. Tools connected with per-user auth act with each teammate's own account, and on the Business and Enterprise plans an audit log records configuration changes. Support for HIPAA compliance is available to eligible Enterprise customers with a BAA and supported configuration.
