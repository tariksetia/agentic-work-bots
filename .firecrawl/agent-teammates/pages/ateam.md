01 · The problem

Ask your team what Monday looks like: reconciling syndicated and retail data so the review deck ships. Meanwhile demand is breaking somewhere your budget is not.

Too late

Fifteen people and most of two weeks go into building the deck, so nobody is left looking for the waste it describes.

Drift

Every market and brand defines the numbers its own way, so the same question gets three answers and none of them travel.

Stuck

### Pilots that never scale

The proofs of concept work. They just never reach the systems and workflows the business actually runs on.

06 · FAQ

## The questions buying committees ask

### Why not bring in a consultancy?

We are applied AI, not an SI or a consultancy, and the part you need most is the part they avoid. A consultancy leaves you a roadmap and a team that rolls off. A systems integrator leaves software your own people cannot evolve. Neither one sits inside your environment and does the last-mile integration that decides whether any of it gets used. Our engineers build the working system where it has to run, stay through production, and hand over the repo, the connectors and the knowledge. You end up with a capability, not a binder.

### We've had pilots that never scaled. Why is this different?

Because the pilot and the production system are the same system. We build inside your environment from day one, against KPIs you set in week one, on the data and tools you already run. There is no rebuild between proving it and running it, and nothing to migrate when you take it over. What usually kills a pilot is the gap between the demo environment and the real one. There isn't one here.

### Do we have to replace what we already have?

No. The teammates sit between the systems you already run rather than replacing them, and connect to the data platforms and model providers you already invested in. What you have already bought starts returning more, instead of being written off.

### How hard is it to leave?

One email. You keep everything we built: the repo, the connectors, and the decision record, which exports to plain text you can hand to any model. We think being easy to leave is the strongest reason to stay.
