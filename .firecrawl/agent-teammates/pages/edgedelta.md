AI Teammates learn from past behavior to understand what “normal” looks like across services, so humans can focus on alerts that matter instead of false positives.

By correlating signals and assigning adaptive criticality scores, AI Teammates help engineers spend less time on manual triage and more time on the work that moves products forward.
