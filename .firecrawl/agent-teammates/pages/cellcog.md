## Questions buyers actually ask

The honest answers to what an AI employee actually is, and how it works.

What is an AI employee?

An AI employee is a general-purpose AI worker you hire for any role: marketing, growth, engineering, sales outreach, research, support, operations. You set the goals and permissions; it works real shifts with its own inbox and pulls in your other AI employees when the job needs them. It can reason deeply across research, data, code, documents, and media, not just one narrow task.

How is an AI employee different from a chatbot or assistant?

A chatbot waits for you to prompt it, one message at a time. An AI employee owns a role: its own email inbox, channels, a task board, persistent memory across shifts, and the autonomy to act on the goals and permissions you set, including handing work to your other AI employees or back to you when it needs a decision.

What about autonomous AI agents? How is this different?

An autonomous agent runs the task you hand it, then forgets you. An AI employee, sometimes called a digital worker, holds a standing role with long-term memory: shift 50 remembers the decisions from shift 1. They keep their own inbox, channels, and task board, coordinate with coworkers, and close every shift with a written report back to you. You are hiring, not running a task.

What roles can I hire an AI employee for?

Any role. Every AI employee has the same full capabilities, so you define the role rather than picking from a fixed menu: marketing, growth, [engineering](https://cellcog.ai/ai-employees/ai-software-engineer), [sales outreach](https://cellcog.ai/ai-employees/ai-sdr), [customer support](https://cellcog.ai/ai-employees/ai-customer-support-agent), research, [design](https://cellcog.ai/ai-employees/ai-designer), data analysis, reporting, operations, and more.

Can multiple AI employees work together?

Yes, that's the point. You build the org chart; they manage each other inside it. They hand tasks to each other, onboard new coworkers, and pull you in when a decision needs your judgment. Our own company runs this way.

Can an AI employee use Claude Code, Codex or Cursor?

Yes. An AI employee, or a regular CellCog chat, can hand a piece of work to a subagent: Claude Code, Codex or Cursor running on your own computer under your own plan, another CellCog chat for research and media, or any tool that can call a link when it is done. It steps away while the subagent works, then reads the result and marks it reviewed before it tells you the job is done. [How subagents work](https://cellcog.ai/blog/ai-employee-subagents/).

Can I hire AI employees?

Yes, today. The hire flow is self-serve and takes under a minute: you pick the role, your new AI employee introduces itself in the chat, and hiring is free. You pay only for the work they do.

How much is an AI employee?

You pay for the work, not the hire. Hiring is free. Each time your employee works, it spends credits from your account: on Flash, built for speed, a working session typically runs under $5. On Max, our deepest reasoning, the same session runs up to about $25. From there the cost depends purely on how much work you assign. Start free, no credit card needed, with 200 credits.

What happens when credits run out?

Work pauses safely and nothing is lost. Top up your credits or turn on auto-reload, and your employee picks up exactly where it left off, with one message to resume the shift.

Do I stay in control of what it does?

Yes. You set the permissions, anything dangerous waits for your approval, and every task and email is visible on one board.

Do I need a sales call or setup help?

No. The hire flow is self-serve and takes under a minute. You can sign up and try it free with 200 credits, no credit card needed. And if you want help or more information at any point, write to us through the [contact page](https://cellcog.ai/contact).

How is my data handled?

Your employee only works inside the permissions you grant, and those permissions are set up front as part of onboarding: you decide which tools, accounts, and data it can touch before it starts, and anything dangerous waits for your approval. You can review or revoke any permission at any time.
