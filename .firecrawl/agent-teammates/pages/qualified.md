## FAQ

## What is an AI SDR?

An AI SDR is like a digital sales rep that never clocks out. It qualifies leads, sends follow-ups, books meetings, and keeps your pipeline humming with no breaks, no burnout, no paycheck required. They are perfect for teams looking to scale without overwhelming their human reps.

## How do AI SDR tools compare to human SDRs?

Human SDRs shine when it comes to building relationships and navigating complex sales. AI SDRs? They crush the repetitive stuff. Think fast email outreach, lead qualification, and instant follow-ups, which frees up your team to focus on closing deals, not chasing them.

## What’s the difference between AI SDRs and chatbots?

Chatbots stick to rigid scripts. AI SDRs adapt. They use context, personalize their outreach, and take real action like booking meetings or re-engaging cold leads. One responds. The other delivers.

## Do AI SDRs integrate with my CRM and marketing tools?

Absolutely. The top AI SDRs plug right into your existing tools like Salesforce, HubSpot, Marketo, Outreach, you name it. This means your team can unlock AI power without rebuilding your whole stack.

## How fast can I get an AI SDR up and running?

Most AI SDRs are designed for quick setup, often within a few weeks. Once connected to your CRM and given some guardrails and your company’s messaging, they’re ready to start qualifying leads and booking meetings almost immediately.

## What kind of results can I expect from an AI SDR?

Faster speed to lead, higher response rates, 24/7 website coverage, fewer dropped leads, and more booked meetings with qualified buyers without hiring more reps.

## How do AI SDRs handle lead quality?

They follow your rules. AI SDRs qualify leads based on your criteria like company size, intent signals, or CRM data so that only the right prospects make it to your team.

## Are AI SDRs compliant with privacy laws?

The good ones are. Look for AI SDR tools that support GDPR, CCPA, and other privacy frameworks and that give you full control over messaging, data, and compliance settings.
