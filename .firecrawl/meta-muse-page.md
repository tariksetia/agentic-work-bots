## FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

* * *

### What can Muse do?

Muse can answer questions, complete tasks, [browse the web](https://www.meta.com/help/2124746764949121/), make purchases, generate images, create documents, and connect with your favorite [apps and services](https://www.meta.com/help/1687253048996149/) via [Connectors](http://www.meta.com/help/1687253048996149). It can also set reminders, track goals, and monitor things you care about, all working in the background on your behalf. Learn [how we designed Muse](https://introducing.muse.ai/).

* * *

### How does Muse work?

Because it’s [agentic AI](https://ai.meta.com/learn/agentic-ai/what-is-agentic-ai/), when you give Muse a task, it figures out which [built-in skills](https://www.meta.com/help/2797651547267109/) it needs and gets to work. It can browse the web, pull information from connected apps, and take action across multiple steps. You stay in control by setting permissions, reviewing and approving what it does along the way.

* * *

### Is Muse free?

Muse is available for free with a usage limit. If you reach your free limit and want more, you can upgrade to a paid subscription, or wait until your free usage limit refreshes.

* * *

### Is Muse safe?

Yes. Muse is built with automated protections, agentic AI security standards, and [user controls](https://www.meta.com/help/1047255454427887) designed to prevent harmful content and unauthorized actions. It also uses a protective layer to stop your information from being shared without your approval. You can [review your agent's activity](https://www.meta.com/help/1385290430137537), manage permissions, and report issues at any time.

* * *

### Does Muse keep working after I close the app?

Yes. When you ask Muse to do something, it keeps working in the background whether you're actively using the app or not. For example, if you ask it to monitor daily weather reports and alert you about rain, it will do that on its own without you needing to check in.

* * *

### Does Muse ask before it acts on my behalf?

Yes. Muse is designed to ask your permission before taking certain actions, like sending messages, making purchases, or sharing information with a connected app. You can choose to allow an action once, always allow it, or deny it. You can manage your [default permission settings](https://www.meta.com/help/1385290430137537/) for every connection at any time.

* * *

### What is a personal AI agent?

A personal [AI agent](https://ai.meta.com/learn/agentic-ai/what-are-ai-agents/) is an AI that can take actions and get things done on your behalf, not just answer questions. Muse, for example, can browse the web, connect to your apps, complete multi-step tasks, and keep working in the background with your direction and oversight.

* * *

### How is a personal agent different from a chatbot or AI assistant?

A chatbot responds to one question at a time. An AI assistant can handle more complex conversations and generate content. A personal AI agent like Muse goes further: it can take real actions across the web and connected apps, work through multi-step tasks on its own, and keep running in the background even after you close the app.

* * *

### What can a personal AI agent help with?

A personal AI agent can help with everyday tasks like booking reservations, monitoring prices, managing reminders, creating documents, generating images, and researching topics. It can also connect to third-party apps and services to take actions on your behalf, like sending emails or completing online purchases. See more [agentic AI examples](https://ai.meta.com/learn/agentic-ai/agentic-ai-examples/).
